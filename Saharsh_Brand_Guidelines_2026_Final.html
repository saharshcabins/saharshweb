Brand Architecture
Three pillars.
One brand.

Every piece of communication — from an Instagram caption to an international sales deck — must trace back to one of these three pillars. No exceptions.

01
Primary
Institutional Credibility

"Trusted where the standards are highest." We have been validated against the most demanding international hospitality specifications in the world. Lead with it. In every context. Always. This credential collapses price objections and shortens sales cycles.

02
Primary
Manufacturing Precision at Scale

"Four facilities. One standard. No exceptions." Frame this as a capability statement, not a list of cities. We have genuine manufacturing infrastructure capable of delivering pan-India and internationally.

03
Primary
Design-Forward Rapid Deployment

"From blueprint to check-in in 10 weeks." Always frame speed in terms of revenue for the buyer — not construction efficiency. Every empty room before opening is lost money we help them recover.

Brand promise — use verbatim
"Built for the extraordinary. Delivered in weeks." — This is our tagline. It anchors every headline, every pitch, every communication. It is not optional.

Colour System
A palette
built for
luxury.

Version 2.0 establishes a single unified palette. Warm Gold and Dark Stone are the only primary colours. There are no tiers, no variants, no exceptions.

Colour roles
Warm Gold — accent, credential, CTA
Dark Stone — dominant background
Stone Surface — card depth
White Linen — neutral base
Green Moss — eco accent only
Primary palette
Warm Gold
#C9A96E
The luxury signal. Credential pills, CTAs, rule lines, icon fills, italic display text, stat numbers. Used as accent — never as a background except in the CTA band.
Dark Stone
#0F1B2D
The authority anchor. Navigation, hero, advantage section, contact, footer. Primary text on linen backgrounds. The brand's dominant presence.
Supporting palette
White Linen
#F5F2EC
Primary neutral. Page backgrounds, section alternation, card surfaces. Warmer than pure white — never use #FFFFFF as a page background.
Stone Surface
#1E2F45
Secondary dark. Card backgrounds within dark sections. Never use as a standalone page background — only as depth layering on Stone.
Green Moss
#3D5A3E
Sustainability accent only. Used in the eco band on the website and eco-resort collateral. Never as a primary colour or section background.
What happened to Orange Clay?

Orange Clay (#C8622A) was the original Saharsh brand colour. In Version 2.0, it is formally retired from active use. It may be referenced in our brand heritage story but does not appear in any live collateral — digital or print. Warm Gold replaces its role as the primary accent, at a higher luxury tier.

Logo
The Saharsh
mark.

The logo consists of a geometric modular mark — referencing the precision and modularity of our manufacturing — paired with the wordmark "Saharsh" in SemiBold and "Cabins" in Light weight.

Clear space rule

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "S" in Saharsh on all four sides. The logo must never be crowded, reversed in unauthorised colours, or placed on a busy photographic background without a dark overlay.

Brand Voice
How we
speak.
Confident
We state facts. We don't qualify with unnecessary hedges. We have earned the right to be direct.
Precise
Specific numbers over vague claims. "10–12 weeks" not "rapid". "11,000 sq. ft." not "large".
Elevated
We speak the language of luxury hospitality. "Retreats" not "cabins". "Handover" not "delivery". "Precision" not "quality".
Never
Boastful · Generic · Apologetic about premium pricing · "Leading" or "world-class" without proof
Voice in practice — do and don't
✓ Say this
"We are the only Indian manufacturer whose work has been showcased at the Lodging Conference in Phoenix — and passed international hospitality brand specifications on first evaluation."
✗ Not this
"We are a leading manufacturer of high-quality modular cabins with 15 years of experience delivering premium solutions to discerning clients."
✓ Say this
"Your property is guest-ready in 10–12 weeks. Every empty room before then is lost revenue. We close that gap faster than any alternative."
✗ Not this
"Our quick turnaround times and competitive pricing make us the best choice for your modular construction needs."
✓ Say this
"We are not the cheapest option in modular. We are the right option for operators who cannot afford to compromise on guest experience."
✗ Not this
"We offer affordable modular solutions for all budgets without compromising on quality."

Photography
Images that
speak first.

Photography is the most powerful brand signal after the colour palette. Every image used in Saharsh collateral must be consistent with the luxury hospitality tier we operate at. Stock photography is never acceptable.

Photography direction
✓ Shoot for this
Wide architectural shots at golden hour. Close-up material and finish details. Interior mood shots with warm ambient light. Behind-the-scenes manufacturing precision — slow, deliberate, close-up. Project walkthroughs from raw site to final handover.
✗ Never use
Smiling salesperson photos. Generic modular construction site imagery. Bright, oversaturated property developer photography. Clip art or AI-generated imagery. Photos of budget or utility units in any context.
